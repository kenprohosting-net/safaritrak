Privacy Policy

Last updated 4 August 2026

This page explains what information SafariTrak collects, why we collect it, and how it is used. We built SafariTrak around your safety, so we take this seriously.

1. Information we collect

  • Account details you give us, such as your name, phone number and email address
  • Your live location while a journey is active, and your journey history
  • Messages you send through SafariTrak to your trusted contacts
  • Information about your trusted contacts, such as their name and phone number

2. How we use your information

We use your information to run the core features of SafariTrak: showing your route, tracking your journey, sharing your location with the people you choose, sending safety alerts, and letting you message your trusted contacts.

3. Who can see your information

Your live location and journey status are only visible to the trusted contacts you specifically choose to share them with. We do not sell your personal information to advertisers or other third parties.

4. SOS and emergency alerts

When you trigger an SOS alert, your current location is shared with your trusted contacts so they can help you or contact emergency services on your behalf.

5. How long we keep your data

We keep your journey history so you can look back on past trips. You can delete individual journeys or your entire account at any time from Settings.

6. Your choices

  • You can turn off location sharing at any time
  • You can remove a trusted contact whenever you want
  • You can request a copy of your data or delete your account from Settings

7. Keeping your data safe

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your information. No system is completely secure, so we encourage you to use a strong password and keep your login details private.

8. Changes to this policy

If we make significant changes to how we handle your information, we will notify you within the app before the changes take effect.

9. Contact us

If you have questions about your privacy on SafariTrak, you can reach the SafariTrak team through the Help Center in your account settings.
