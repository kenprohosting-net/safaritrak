These terms explain what you are agreeing to when you use SafariTrak. Please read them so you know what to expect from us and what we expect from you.

1. What SafariTrak is

SafariTrak helps you plan, navigate and track your journeys, share your location with people you trust, and get help through safety tools like route deviation alerts and the SOS button. It is a travel companion, not a replacement for emergency services.

2. Your account

You need an account to use SafariTrak. You are responsible for keeping your login details safe and for anything that happens under your account. Tell us right away if you think someone else has access to it.

3. Location and journey data

To track your journeys and share your location, SafariTrak needs access to your device location while a journey is active. You control who can see this information through your trusted contacts, trip and organization permissions, and you can stop sharing or leave a trip where those controls are available. Location features depend on device settings, GPS, connectivity and battery. A map marker may be delayed or inaccurate.

4. Trusted contacts and sharing

When you add someone as a trusted contact, you authorize access to the information you choose to share, such as your live location, journey status or SOS alerts. Group trip participation also shares location while the group trip is active with its authorized members. Only add people you trust, and do not use SafariTrak to monitor anyone without their knowledge and consent.

5. Organizations and school trips

Organization administrators may invite people to join groups or view specific trip information. School membership alone does not grant access to a student's personal journeys or live location. A guardian must accept a student-specific trip invitation before that access is enabled, and a parent or guardian should use child-related access only when authorized to do so and promptly notify the school if access is incorrect. Organizations are responsible for managing their invitations, group names and membership.

6. Car hire and place listings

Car hire listings, company profiles, vehicle details, prices, availability, pickup locations and rental policies are provided by the relevant business and may be incomplete or out of date. SafariTrak does not verify that a company-provided policy is a government-approved requirement. A booking or quotation request is an enquiry only: it is not a confirmed reservation or payment. Confirm terms, availability, safety, insurance, driver eligibility and all costs directly with the provider before travel.

Place results and saved places may come from third-party mapping providers or user-entered information. Check addresses, opening hours and safety information independently before relying on them.

7. Safety tools have limits

Features like route deviation alerts and SOS notifications depend on your device having a working internet connection, GPS signal and battery. SafariTrak cannot guarantee these tools will always work, so please do not rely on them as your only safety measure in an emergency.

8. Acceptable use

  • Do not use SafariTrak to track or monitor someone without their knowledge or consent
